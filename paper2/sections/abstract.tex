\begin{abstract}
We construct a smooth projective Calabi--Yau threefold with torsion-free
second homology whose product expansion in Pardon's scalar equivariant
local-curve generators has a nonintegral exponent. The genus-ten exponent
at three times a primitive class has fractional part $2/3$, disproving
the integer-exponent assertion for these generators. The obstruction
comes from an exact degree-three calculation in the local
Gromov--Witten Frobenius algebra. Comparison with the integer elementary
expansion of Ionel--Parker gives a necessary divisibility condition on
the primitive genus-three count. We realize a count violating this
condition on a first Hirzebruch surface in a compact toric hypersurface.
A filtration by powers of the surface ideal controls transverse
thickenings and disconnected products; the stable-pair moduli space at
minimal Euler characteristic is a projective space of dimension thirteen, with virtual count
$-14$. We also prove integrality in classes of divisibility at most two.
The counterexample concerns the scalar local factors; the integer
elementary expansion is an input to its proof.
\end{abstract}
