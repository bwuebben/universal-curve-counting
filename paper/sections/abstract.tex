\begin{abstract}
For projective conifold transitions of Calabi--Yau threefolds, we lift
the numerical degeneration formula to Pardon's rational universal
curve-counting algebra. We assume a smoothing with smooth algebraic
total space and torsion-free second homology at both endpoints.
After removing the exceptional local-curve factors, effective lifts
to the common blow-up give a support bound that makes specialization
finite in every bounded target degree. We carry out this
specialization completely in the primitive line class of a plane
in the smoothing of a compact four-node transition. The source calculation uses an
Euler-characteristic bound for pure curves on two intersecting rational
surfaces, allowing arbitrary multiple exceptional components.
Together with a finite genus-zero mirror calculation, this bound
determines all 37 candidate universal coefficients: eight are
nonzero, and their sum agrees with an independent stable-pair
calculation on a plane in the smoothing. We also determine the
smoothing coefficient in twice the line class. If the resolution has
integral product exponents, then so does the smoothing.
\end{abstract}
