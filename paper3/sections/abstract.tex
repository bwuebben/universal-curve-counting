\begin{abstract}
Assuming Pardon's Gromov--Witten evaluation for analytic families of
threefolds, we lift the elementary-cluster expansion of Ionel and
Parker to his rational algebra of one-cycles. For every smooth
projective Calabi--Yau threefold the universal series factors uniquely
with the elementary-cluster integers as exponents, indexed by effective
classes in integral homology. We construct the genus-$g$ factor using
the volume-preserving circle in the torus that scales the two line
summands of a balanced local curve. Symmetric functions of the two
fiber projections isolate the multiples of the zero section and give
compactly supported classes compatible with addition of cycles and
multiplication by positive integers. Among series with these
compatibilities, the local
Gromov--Witten series of an elementary cluster determines the
universal factor. Among split local Calabi--Yau curves with the
antidiagonal action, agreement with the elementary cluster in
degree two already forces the splitting degrees to be balanced.
\end{abstract}
