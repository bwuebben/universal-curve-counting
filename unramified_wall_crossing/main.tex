\begin{abstract}
Let two independent unramified double covers of an embedded curve acquire
normal deformations along transverse walls. We construct such a family
over every curve of genus at least two with a complex rank-two normal
bundle of degree twice the genus minus two. The alternating difference of
the local Gromov--Witten invariants in its four chambers equals the local
invariant on the common fourfold cover, in every source genus.
At each fixed covering-degree bound one neighborhood suffices for all genera.
On a finite principal unramified cover we realize simultaneous crossings
for pairwise inequivalent nontrivial irreducible representations of real
type, provided their associated flat bundles together embed in the
underlying real normal bundle. Fixed vectors determine the signs, and the
result is a sum of local invariants on intermediate covers. Along a path
of these crossings the determinant signs transport the initial series on
the covers in all genera. For arbitrary rigid endpoints, the signs at
both endpoints determine the change in the least source genus, the genus
of an unramified cover of the given degree.
\end{abstract}
