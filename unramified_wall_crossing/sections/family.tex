\section{Families with prescribed kernels on an unramified cover}\label{sec:family}
Let \(C\) be a fixed smooth compact complex curve of genus \(g\ge2\) and \(N\) a complex plane bundle of degree \(2g-2\). Fix a connected unramified principal \(G\)-cover \(Z\to C\) for a finite group \(G\). Choose distinct, nontrivial, irreducible real representations \(V_1,\dots,V_r\), up to isomorphism, with \(\operatorname{End}_G(V_i)=\mathbb R\). Write \(V=\bigoplus_i V_i\). We may take its action to be faithful:
if \(K\) is its kernel, use the principal \(G/K\)-cover \(Z/K\to C\).
The associated bundles on \(C\) are unchanged. Inflation carries each
orbit \([(G/K)/(H/K)]\) to \([G/H]\), with \(K\subset H\); both
give the same intermediate cover \(Z/H\). For any subgroup \(J\)
the fixed space is \(V^{JK/K}=V^J\). Hence the orbit formula and its
marks pull back to the original group. Suppose the associated real bundle \(E=Z\times_G V\) admits a fiberwise real monomorphism in \(N\). This forces \(\dim_{\mathbb R}V\le4\). Orthogonal metrics identify each representation with its dual. The crossing parameters below vary real Cauchy--Riemann operators on the \emph{same} curve and normal bundle.

For a subgroup \(H\le G\) put \(T_H=Z/H\) and \(a_H=[G:H]\). At a regular operator through degree \(M\), \(L^C_{d,h}\) is the connected fixed-target local Gromov--Witten degree in covering degree \(d\le M\) and arithmetic source genus \(h\). Write \(P_C\) for the integral balanced lift of the local insertion through \(M\), normalized by constant coefficient one. On \(T_H\) use the pulled-back operator and the induced cutoff \(\lfloor M/a_H\rfloor\). The balanced factors and their degreewise uniqueness are established in Proposition~\ref{prop:factors}. Their integral classes are identified by the basis theorem of \cite[Theorem~1.1]{WueGenerators}.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:mixed} There is a smooth family $D_{\mathbf t}$ for $\mathbf t$ near $0\in\mathbb R^r$ in which the $r$ twisted kernel lines cross independently. For every finite $M$ there is a box about zero on whose open orthants all pullbacks to positive-degree connected stable maps of degree at most $M$ are injective, without restriction on source genus. On a coordinate face the entire kernel on such a map is the flat fixed subspace of the summands whose coordinates vanish. One may use the same disk tube for the marked curve at all regular parameters in this box. The compact local insertion there is its pure multiple in each degree.

Let $I$ be a nonempty subset of the coordinates. Fix signs outside $I$. Use $+$ on all coordinates of $I$ to designate the reference corner $*$. For $J\subset I$, write $*J$ for this corner with the signs indexed by $J$ reversed. The values at points of a given open orthant agree throughout the box. Set
\[
 B_I=\prod_{i\in I}\chi^G(S(V_i))
    =\sum_{(H)}b_{I,H}[G/H]\quad\text{in }A(G).
\]
Here $S(V_i)$ denotes the unit sphere, and $A(G)$ is the Grothendieck ring of finite $G$-sets with Cartesian product. The equivariant Euler characteristic uses cell orbits counted with their alternating signs. Then
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 \sum_{J\subset I}(-1)^{|J|}L^C_{d,h}(*J)
    &=\sum_{(H):\,a_H\mid d}b_{I,H}L^{T_H}_{d/a_H,h}(*),\\
 \prod_{J\subset I}P_C(*J;q)^{(-1)^{|J|}}
    &=\prod_{(H)}P_{T_H}(*;q^{a_H})^{b_{I,H}}
            \pmod {q^{M+1}}.
\end{aligned}\label{eq:mixed}
\end{equation}
Shrinking the box as $M$ grows defines one series for each orthant as a germ in the degree completion. An individual nonzero parameter realizes the coefficients through the cutoff of its box.

\end{theorem}

\subsection{Simultaneous twisted kernels}\label{simultaneous-twisted-kernels}

Write \(E_i=Z\times_G V_i\). At the center we prescribe parallel vectors in each \(E_i\) as solutions via a monomorphism \(S:E\to N_{\mathbb R}\). Start with any reference operator \(D_*\). For a local parallel section \(v\) of \(E\), prescribe a real zeroth-order correction on the image by \(Q(Sv)=-D_*(Sv)\) and extend it along a complement. This defines a global pair \((D,S)\) with \(D^E S=0\). The section and operator may both vary. We give the varying-section argument because four real columns can span the whole normal fiber.

A chart of pairs chooses a nearby monomorphism and freely prescribes the operator correction on a complementary subbundle. For any disk of regular values, a pair with an infinite-order Petri jet at a chosen inner point is dense. Perturb the operator on the disk by the unrestricted jet theorem. For each parallel column take a close solution of the new equation on a smaller disk using the small-disk Cauchy transform. Interpolate each solution and the operator back to the original data with cutoffs. The interpolated monomorphism \(S'\) is still fiberwise injective; correcting the interpolated operator \(D'\) by \(-(D'^{E}S')J\), where \(JS'=1\), restores all prescribed equations and leaves the inner disk intact. These choices approximate the initial pair in any specified finite set of smooth seminorms \cite[Definition~5.1 and Corollary~5.10]{wendl}.

Consider an additional twisted solution \(u\) for a finite-monodromy irreducible real local system \(U\) with \(s\) distinct effective punctures. Use its orbifold extension at the branch values. If \(U=E_i\) restrict to \(u\) orthogonal to the one prescribed intertwiner \(S_i\). On a common connected normal cover the columns of \(S\) give one copy of every \(V_i\). The columns of a nonzero \(u\) form an irreducible subrepresentation. An intersection with their direct sum would put this whole subrepresentation inside it. Its projection to each inequivalent summand would vanish, and in its own type the intertwiner would be a real multiple of \(S_i\). This contradicts the complement condition. Thus the full list of columns is real linearly independent, including as germs by unique continuation.

At a Petri point away from the branch values the joint section equations for \((S,u)\) have surjective derivative when the operator is allowed to vary. In fact an annihilator solves the local adjoint equations and unrestricted variations of the operator give the identity \(\sum s_j\otimes\alpha_j+\sum u_l\otimes\eta_l=0\) on the disk. Independence of input germs and the Petri condition force the adjoint germs to vanish. Unique continuation on the normal covers removes the annihilator. The Fredholm section derivatives have closed images of finite codimension. Projecting onto the second equation on the tangent space where the first equations are satisfied is therefore surjective. In a distinguished type one may adjust \(\dot u\) in the \(S_i\) direction to maintain orthogonality to the moving prescribed intertwiner.

For this orbifold system the real index is \(-2\sum_x\operatorname{codim}U^{I_x}\), where \(I_x\) is local inertia. The projectivized incidence index after moving branch values is at most \(2s-1-2\sum_x\operatorname{codim}U^{I_x}\le-1\). Removing the prescribed direction in a distinguished type decreases the index further. On each compact block of branch configurations avoid the branch values with a common disk. Normalized solutions over that block form a compact set by elliptic estimates on its smooth family of normal covers. At a pair having a Petri point there, finitely many smooth tangent perturbations of the pair suffice for surjectivity near all of these solutions. The chart of pairs integrates these variations. Sard's theorem on the resulting negative-relative-index incidence removes the extraneous solutions; their absence over the block is open by the same compactness. A countable exhaustion over configurations and finite monodromy systems gives a pair \((D_0,S)\) with no other irreducible kernels. This uses the orbifold index and removability as in \cite[\S2.8]{dwbn}.

For every smooth finite cover \(u:X\to C\), with any branching, passage to a common normal cover and descent now give \(\ker u^*D_0=S(V^{K_u})\) in parallel frames. Here \(K_u\le G\) is the monodromy image of \(u^*Z\), defined up to conjugacy. A contracted component of a stable map has constant normal sections. Every connected contracted subcurve meets a positive component. At every node fiberwise injectivity of \(S\) identifies matching values with matching parallel vectors. Thus the same result holds for the full connected nodal domain, including its graph loops.

\subsection{Transverse parameters and control on a whole face}

The twisted operator for each \(E_i\) has index zero by Riemann--Roch and has kernel and cokernel of real dimension one. Choose a variation \(Q_i\) killing every other \(S_j\) and pairing nontrivially on \(S_i\) with its twisted adjoint. On a disk where the adjoint is nonzero, injectivity of \(S\) permits independent choices on all the real columns, hence this prescription. Put \(D_{\mathbf t}=D_0+\sum_i t_i Q_i\). In the \(i\)th twisted kernel equation the real scalar after Lyapunov--Schmidt reduction is \(t_i\) times a nonvanishing smooth function: it vanishes identically on \(t_i=0\) since \(S_i\) survives there, and its normal derivative at zero is nonzero. On \(Z\) the other isotypic blocks stay invertible. Indeed the \(V_i\) blocks already account for the full cokernel at zero by their index calculation, equivariance and the index zero on \(Z\). Exactness of taking \(H\)-invariants gives the base kernel on every fixed quotient \(T_H\) for small parameters:
\begin{equation}
\ker D_{\mathbf t}|_{T_H}\simeq\bigoplus_{i:t_i=0}V_i^H.                \label{eq:face-kernels}
\end{equation}
These kernels are represented by the fixed columns \(S_i\).

For completeness, \eqref{eq:face-kernels} on finitely many quotients implies a uniform assertion on all smooth degree-bounded covers, even along the punctured faces. Otherwise choose parameters tending to the origin on one face (pass to a subsequence with the same zero coordinates \(F\)). On offending smooth connected covers \(u_l\) of fixed degree \(d\le M\), select an extra solution. Subtract a best approximation in sup norm by the finite-dimensional space \(K_l\) of parallel sections of \(u_l^*(\oplus_{i\in F}E_i)\), evaluated via the pulled-back \(S\). This space is closed and a minimizer exists by finite dimensionality. Rescale the resulting representatives so their sup norm and their distance from \(K_l\) both equal a fixed small \(\rho>0\).

The normalized graphs lie in one compact disk subbundle. In the graph almost-complex structures a common taming form there is the pullback of base area plus the exact differential of the vertical Liouville form. Their areas, with mapping multiplicities, are \(d\) times the base area. Use the compact embedding from the proof of Proposition~\ref{prop:factors} on a slightly larger tube. The graphs stay in the inner disk, so cycle compactness on the closed ambient six-torus gives a connected central limit support in the Hausdorff topology meeting the radius-\(\rho\) sphere. Each simple component is a graph over a positive-degree cover. By the central classification, its image is the embedded orbit graph of some \(v\in V\), on the target \(T_H\) with \(H=\operatorname{Stab}(v)\). Distinct \(G\)-orbits have disjoint compact graph images by fiberwise injectivity. The finite union in the limiting support is connected and therefore consists of one such graph, with \(v\ne0\). This is bounded-area cycle compactness, independent of genus \cite[Theorem~2.11]{diw}.

The sheets of this fixed graph have positive minimum separation whenever there is more than one sheet. Taking the nearest sheet gives a holomorphic lift \(a_l:X_l\to T_H\) of \(u_l\) and the sections are uniformly close to \(\sigma_v\circ a_l\). For a single sheet just use \(u_l\). Average each section over the sheets of \(a_l\). The average solves \(D_{\mathbf t_l}|_{T_H}\) by real linearity and extends over branch points by boundedness, the weak equation and elliptic regularity. The averages converge uniformly to \(\sigma_v\). By \eqref{eq:face-kernels} they lie in one fixed closed subspace, so \(v\in\oplus_{i\in F}V_i^H\). The pulled-back approximation \(\sigma_v\circ a_l\) therefore belongs to \(K_l\). Its distance from the normalized sections tends to zero, contradicting the distance \(\rho\). Nodal matching gives the asserted classifications on connected stable maps. This proof applies to the finitely many faces at once. In open orthants no extra support can occur in the disk tube: normalization of any nonzero compact curve would give a kernel graph, and a vector space fiber contains no compact curve. The fixed tube identifies the markings at regular parameters; at the central parameter entire dilation orbits may meet its boundary.
